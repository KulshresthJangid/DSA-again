# Sum of Two Integers

**Pattern**: Bit Manipulation | **Difficulty**: Medium
**LeetCode**: [#371. Sum of Two Integers](https://leetcode.com/problems/sum-of-two-integers/)

---

## Like You're 5
Add without using + or -.
XOR gives you the sum bits without carry. AND gives you the carry bits. Shift carry left (carries add to next position). Repeat until no carry.

---

## The Real Problem
Calculate a+b without + or - operators.

---

## How to Solve It
```
while b != 0:
    carry = a & b        # bits that carry
    a = a ^ b            # sum without carry
    b = carry << 1       # carry shifts left
return a
```
(In Python, handle 32-bit overflow mask)

---

## The One Trick
> "XOR = addition without carry. AND+shift = the carries. Repeat until no carries."

**Time**: O(1) — max 32 iterations | **Space**: O(1)
