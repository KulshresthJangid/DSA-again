# Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

**Pattern**: Sliding Window / Greedy | **Difficulty**: Easy

---

## Like You're 5
Imagine prices of candy go up and down every day. You can buy once and sell once.
You want to buy on the cheapest day *before* the most expensive day.
Walk through the prices left to right. Keep track of the lowest price you've seen so far. At each price, ask "if I sell today, how much profit do I make?"

---

## The Real Problem
Given daily prices, find max profit from one buy and one sell (buy must come before sell).

**Example**: `[7,1,5,3,6,4]` → `5` (buy at 1, sell at 6)

---

## How to Solve It
1. Start: `minPrice = infinity`, `maxProfit = 0`
2. For each price:
   - Update `minPrice = min(minPrice, price)`
   - Update `maxProfit = max(maxProfit, price - minPrice)`
3. Return `maxProfit`

---

## See It Happen
```
prices=[7,1,5,3,6,4]
p=7: min=7, profit=0
p=1: min=1, profit=0
p=5: min=1, profit=4
p=3: min=1, profit=4
p=6: min=1, profit=5 ✓
p=4: min=1, profit=5
```

---

## The One Trick
> "Track the lowest buy price so far — profit at any point is just current price minus that."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(1)
