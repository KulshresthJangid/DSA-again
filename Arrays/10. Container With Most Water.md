# Container With Most Water

**Pattern**: Two Pointers | **Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Like You're 5
You have walls of different heights. Water sits between two walls. The water height = the shorter wall. Move the shorter wall inward — maybe the next wall is taller and holds more water.

---

## The Real Problem
Given heights, find two lines that form a container holding the most water.

**Example**: `[1,8,6,2,5,4,8,3,7]` → `49`

---

## How to Solve It
1. `left=0`, `right=end`, `maxWater=0`
2. While left < right:
   - `water = min(height[left], height[right]) × (right-left)`
   - `maxWater = max(maxWater, water)`
   - Move the pointer with the shorter wall inward

---

## The One Trick
> "Always move the shorter side — the taller side can only help if paired with something better."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(1)
