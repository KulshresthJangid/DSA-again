# Product of Array Except Self

**Pattern**: Prefix & Suffix Products | **Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Like You're 5
You have 4 kids in a line. Each kid wants to know: "what's the product of everyone else's number?"
Kid 2 needs: kid1 × kid3 × kid4.
Trick: give each kid a "left product" (everything to their left multiplied) and a "right product" (everything to their right multiplied). Multiply those two — that's their answer.

---

## The Real Problem
Return array where `output[i]` = product of all elements except `nums[i]`. No division allowed.

**Example**: `[1,2,3,4]` → `[24,12,8,6]`

---

## How to Solve It
1. Build `left[]` where `left[i]` = product of all elements before i
2. Build `right[]` where `right[i]` = product of all elements after i
3. `result[i] = left[i] * right[i]`

(Optimized: do it in one output array, use a running variable for right pass)

---

## See It Happen
```
nums=[1,2,3,4]
left =[1, 1, 2, 6]   (1, 1×1, 1×2, 1×2×3)
right=[24,12, 4, 1]   (2×3×4, 3×4, 4, 1)
res  =[24,12, 8, 6]
```

---

## The One Trick
> "Split the problem: what's to my left? What's to my right? Multiply those two answers."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(1) (excluding output)
