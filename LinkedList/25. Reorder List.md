# Reorder List

**Pattern**: Find Middle + Reverse + Merge | **Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Like You're 5
Take list `1→2→3→4→5`. Reorder to `1→5→2→4→3`.
Steps: find middle, reverse second half, then weave the two halves together.

---

## The Real Problem
Reorder `L0→L1→...→Ln-1→Ln` to `L0→Ln→L1→Ln-1→...`

---

## How to Solve It
1. Find middle (slow/fast pointers)
2. Reverse second half
3. Merge two halves: take one from each alternately

---

## The One Trick
> "Three sub-problems: find middle, reverse half, interleave. Each is a classic you already know."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(1)
