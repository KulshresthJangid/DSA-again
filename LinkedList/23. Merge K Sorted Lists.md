# Merge K Sorted Lists

**Pattern**: Heap (Min-Heap) | **Difficulty**: Hard
**LeetCode**: [#23. Merge K Sorted Lists](https://leetcode.com/problems/merge-k-sorted-lists/)

---

## Like You're 5
You have k sorted piles. Instead of comparing all k top cards every time, use a "magic box" (min-heap) that always shows you the smallest card among all tops instantly.

---

## The Real Problem
Merge k sorted linked lists into one sorted list.

---

## How to Solve It
1. Add head of each list to a min-heap
2. While heap not empty:
   - Pop smallest node, attach to result
   - If that node has a next, push next to heap
3. Return result

---

## The One Trick
> "Min-heap always gives you the global minimum in O(log k). That's the whole trick."

**Time**: O(n log k) | **Space**: O(k)
