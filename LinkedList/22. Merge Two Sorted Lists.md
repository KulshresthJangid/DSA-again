# Merge Two Sorted Lists

**Pattern**: Two Pointer / Merge | **Difficulty**: Easy

---

## Like You're 5
Two sorted piles of numbered cards. Keep picking the smaller top card from either pile and adding to a new pile. When one pile is empty, dump the rest of the other pile.

---

## The Real Problem
Merge two sorted linked lists into one sorted list.

**Example**: `1→2→4` + `1→3→4` → `1→1→2→3→4→4`

---

## How to Solve It
1. Create dummy head node
2. `curr = dummy`
3. While both lists have nodes:
   - Pick smaller value, attach to curr, advance that list
4. Attach remaining list
5. Return `dummy.next`

---

## The One Trick
> "Dummy head removes edge cases. Always attach the smaller node, advance that pointer."

**Time**: O(n+m) | **Space**: O(1)
