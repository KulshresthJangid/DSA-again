# Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters

**Pattern**: Sliding Window | **Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Like You're 5
You're reading a word one letter at a time and keeping a window of unique letters.
When a letter repeats, shrink the window from the left until the repeat is gone.
Track the longest window you've ever had.

---

## The Real Problem
Find the length of the longest substring with no repeated characters.

**Example**: `"abcabcbb"` → `3` ("abc")

---

## How to Solve It
1. `left=0`, `maxLen=0`, `charSet=empty set`
2. For each right pointer:
   - While `s[right]` is in set: remove `s[left]`, left++
   - Add `s[right]` to set
   - `maxLen = max(maxLen, right-left+1)`

---

## See It Happen
```
"abcabcbb"
r=0 a: set={a}, len=1
r=1 b: set={a,b}, len=2
r=2 c: set={a,b,c}, len=3
r=3 a: a in set! remove a(l=0)→l=1, add a. set={b,c,a}, len=3
```

---

## The One Trick
> "Expand right freely, shrink left only when you see a duplicate."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(min(n,26))
