# Longest Palindromic Substring

**Pattern**: Expand Around Center | **Difficulty**: Medium
**LeetCode**: [#5. Longest Palindromic Substring](https://leetcode.com/problems/longest-palindromic-substring/)

---

## Like You're 5
A palindrome reads same both ways. Every palindrome has a center.
Try every possible center (each letter, and each gap between letters). Expand outward as long as both sides match. Track the longest expansion.

---

## The Real Problem
Find the longest palindromic substring.

**Example**: `"babad"` → `"bab"` or `"aba"`

---

## How to Solve It
1. For each center (2n-1 centers for even/odd):
   - Expand left and right while chars match
   - Record length
2. Return the longest

---

## The One Trick
> "Every palindrome grows from its center — try all centers, expand outward."

**Time**: O(n²) | **Space**: O(1)
