# Palindromic Substrings

**Pattern**: Expand Around Center | **Difficulty**: Medium
**LeetCode**: [#647. Palindromic Substrings](https://leetcode.com/problems/palindromic-substrings/)

---

## Like You're 5
Same idea as Longest Palindromic Substring, but instead of finding the longest one, *count* how many palindromes exist total (including single letters).

---

## The Real Problem
Count how many palindromic substrings are in the string.

**Example**: `"abc"` → `3` (a, b, c each count), `"aaa"` → `6`

---

## How to Solve It
1. `count = 0`
2. For each center (each char + each gap):
   - Expand while matching, increment count each valid expansion

---

## The One Trick
> "Same expand-around-center trick — count every successful expansion instead of tracking length."

**Time**: O(n²) | **Space**: O(1)
