# Find Median from Data Stream

**Pattern**: Two Heaps | **Difficulty**: Hard
**LeetCode**: [#295. Find Median from Data Stream](https://leetcode.com/problems/find-median-from-data-stream/)

---

## Like You're 5
Keep two piles: a max-heap of the smaller half, a min-heap of the larger half.
Always balance them (sizes differ by at most 1).
Median = top of larger pile OR average of both tops.

---

## The Real Problem
Design a data structure that supports addNum and findMedian efficiently.

---

## How to Solve It
- `small` = max-heap (lower half), `large` = min-heap (upper half)
- addNum: push to small, balance by moving small's max to large, re-balance sizes
- findMedian: if sizes equal → average of both tops. Else → top of larger heap

---

## The One Trick
> "Two heaps split the data at the median. Max-heap for left, min-heap for right. Keep them balanced."

**Time**: O(log n) add, O(1) median | **Space**: O(n)
