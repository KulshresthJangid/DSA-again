# House Robber II

**Pattern**: DP (run twice, skip ends) | **Difficulty**: Medium

---

## Like You're 5
Same as House Robber but houses are in a circle — first and last house are neighbors!
You can't rob both. So run House Robber twice: once skipping the first house, once skipping the last. Take the max of both.

---

## The Real Problem
Houses in a circle — max robbery, no adjacent.

---

## How to Solve It
1. Run House Robber I on `nums[0:-1]` (skip last)
2. Run House Robber I on `nums[1:]` (skip first)
3. Return max of both

---

## The One Trick
> "Circular = first and last can't both be robbed. Run the linear solution twice on the two possible subsets."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(1)
