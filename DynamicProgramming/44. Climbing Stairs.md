# Climbing Stairs

**Pattern**: Dynamic Programming (Fibonacci) | **Difficulty**: Easy
**LeetCode**: [#70. Climbing Stairs](https://leetcode.com/problems/climbing-stairs/)

---

## Like You're 5
You can take 1 or 2 steps at a time. How many ways to reach step 5?
Ways to reach step n = ways to reach step n-1 (then take 1) + ways to reach n-2 (then take 2).
It's just Fibonacci!

---

## The Real Problem
Count distinct ways to climb n stairs (1 or 2 steps at a time).

**Example**: n=3 → 3 ways: (1+1+1), (1+2), (2+1)

---

## How to Solve It
```
dp[1]=1, dp[2]=2
for i in range(3, n+1):
    dp[i] = dp[i-1] + dp[i-2]
return dp[n]
```
Optimize: just track two variables.

---

## The One Trick
> "Ways to reach n = ways to reach n-1 + ways to reach n-2. It's Fibonacci wearing a disguise."

**Time**: O(n) | **Space**: O(1)
