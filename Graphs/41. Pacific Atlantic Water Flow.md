# Pacific Atlantic Water Flow

**Pattern**: Reverse DFS from edges | **Difficulty**: Medium
**LeetCode**: [#417. Pacific Atlantic Water Flow](https://leetcode.com/problems/pacific-atlantic-water-flow/)

---

## Like You're 5
Water flows from high to low. Pacific is top/left edges, Atlantic is bottom/right edges.
Instead of simulating water going down, reverse it: start from each ocean's border and flow *uphill*.
Cells reachable from both oceans → answer.

---

## The Real Problem
Find all cells where water can flow to both Pacific and Atlantic oceans.

---

## How to Solve It
1. BFS/DFS from all Pacific border cells (flow uphill) → `pacific_reachable`
2. BFS/DFS from all Atlantic border cells (flow uphill) → `atlantic_reachable`
3. Return intersection

---

## The One Trick
> "Reverse the flow direction — run from ocean edges inward. Much simpler than forward simulation."

**Time**: O(m×n) | **Space**: O(m×n)
